Sipling View on GitHub

Stay hydrated,
the friendly way.

A little water-drop buddy walks onto your screen, waits with a glass until you drink up, then heads off. It lives in your menu bar and stays quiet the rest of the time.

Download for macOS Download for Windows

Free and open source. Universal macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows x64 and arm64.

Walk-across buddy

Your avatar floats above every app, even in fullscreen, and only the buddy captures your mouse. Keep working while it waits.

On your schedule

Pick a fixed gap, say every two hours, and set the hours it runs. Overnight it stays quiet.

Reach your goal

Set a daily glass goal and watch the water bar fill. Sipling cheers when you get there.

Your own avatar

Drop in an animated avatar and choose the chime you like. The buddy becomes yours.

Out of your way

It lives in the menu bar or system tray. No dock clutter, no window to manage.

Always up to date

Packaged builds update themselves from GitHub Releases, so you always run the latest.
